Binder admin

Recover your admin data

1. Download a backup

Saves everything this browser holds for this address to a file on your computer, before anything else happens.

2. Bring it back into the admin

Opens www.binder.co.in/restore in a new tab, which adds your Accounts and Projects entries to the shared database and your Orders and Customers back into the admin. Nothing already there is overwritten. Sign in to the admin on www.binder.co.in first.
